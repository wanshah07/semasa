# Hook bank — 25 shapes

25 hook shapes transcribed from a reference series, in five groups. Use a shape as a
scaffold: fill the blanks with real substance, then rewrite until it sounds spoken.

`___` is one blank. A shape with two blanks needs both filled or the sentence collapses.

**Never copy the reference author's filled wording.** The shape is free to use; their
sentences are not.

## How to pick

Pick by **how much you are willing to assert**, not by mood:

| Group | Cards | Asserts | Evidence bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CURIOSITY` | 01–05 | nothing, opens a gap | low, but you owe the answer |
| `TRANSFORM` | 06–10 | a **result** | highest |
| `VULNERABLE` | 11–15 | something about **you** | only your own, true story |
| `BOLD` | 16–20 | an opinion | rises the moment a blank names a real thing |
| `RELATABLE` | 21–25 | nothing | none |

Series order is `CURIOSITY` → `TRANSFORM` → `VULNERABLE` → `BOLD` → `RELATABLE`.
Running a group early asks the reader for something they have not been given yet.

## CURIOSITY (01–05) · badge *Curiosity hooks*

1. `Takde sapa cakap pasal ___ tapi ni sebab aku ___`
2. `Aku ignore ___ berbulan2. Pastu aku try dan ___`
3. `Benda pasal ___ yg takde sapa bagitau kau: ___`
4. `Ni apa sebenarnya jadi bila kau ___`
5. `Aku wish ada org bagitau aku ___ sebelum aku start ___`

Fill: blank 1 is a thing or practice, blank 2 is your reason or result. Shape 4 blank 1 is an
**action**, and the body must actually explain the mechanism.

Guardrail: shape 3 implies someone suppressed the information. On halal, cosmetic or health
topics that reads as an accusation against institutions. Prefer 4 or 5. Never use shape 4 on
a hazard unless the answer is known and citable.

## TRANSFORM (06–10) · badge *Transform hooks*

6. `6 bulan lepas aku x boleh ___. Hari ni aku ___`
7. `Aku dulu fikir ___ mustahil sampai la ___`
8. `Hari aku stop buat ___, everything changed`
9. `Before ___ vs After ___: ni apa yg berubah`
10. `Aku dari ___ ke ___ sbb buat SATU benda ni je`

Fill: blanks hold **states of practice**, never outcomes with numbers. Safe: "x boleh terangkan
beza notifikasi dan kelulusan". Unsafe: any figure, timeframe, approval or cure. Shape 5
promises one lever caused everything, which is the most seductive and least defensible.

## VULNERABLE (11–15) · badge *Vulnerable hooks*

11. `Aku takut nak share ___ tapi ni kenapa penting`
12. `Kegagalan terbesar aku dgn ___ ajar aku ___`
13. `Aku almost give up bila ___ tapi lepas tu ___`
14. `Takde sapa nampak part mana aku ___`
15. `Aku struggle ___ bulan dgn ___ sebelum aku figured out ___`

Fill: only your own experience. Never a client's, a colleague's, or an imagined person's, even
with permission, because the voice is wrong even when the facts are true. The resolution must be
a change in understanding, not an outcome with a number.

This group fails hardest when faked, and it does not survive on a brand account. A company
confessing a failure invites liability without earning intimacy.

## BOLD (16–20) · badge *Bold statement hooks*

16. `Semua org ckp ___ tapi realitinya ___`
17. `Stop buat ___ kalau kau nak ___`
18. `___ dah mati. Ni apa yg actually works skrg`
19. `Sebab sebenar kenapa kau masih x ___ lagi`
20. `Hot take: ___ x penting mana pun compared to ___`

Fill: safe while the blanks stay abstract or generic. Once a blank names a real ingredient,
standard, product or practice, the opinion becomes a claim about that thing. Shape 18 obliges
you to name a real replacement, not just a provocative blank. Shape 19 is the friendliest for a
professional account because it flatters the reader instead of scolding them.

## RELATABLE (21–25) · badge *Relatable struggle*

21. `POV: kau ___ tapi ___`
22. `Feeling tu bila kau dah ___ tapi masih ___`
23. `Aku: esok la aku ___. Also aku: ___`
24. `Cuba nak ___ sambil ___ be like`
25. `Org lain: ___. Aku: masih figuring out ___`

Fill: each blank is something the **reader** does or feels, never something you sell. Shape 22
needs a real contradiction in the two slots. Shape 23 the second half must undercut the first.
Shape 25's punchline is the gap, so do not resolve it.

## Writing checklist

1. Family chosen by risk, not mood.
2. Both blanks filled, specific enough to be interesting, general enough to stay true.
3. Read it aloud. If it sounds like translated English, rewrite it.
4. Every claim checkable by a client or regulator either has substance or goes back to a blank.
5. The body closes whatever the hook promised.
6. No em dash. No invented number, name, quote or result.
