# English new style

Reference: `reference/reference.mp4` (26.0 s, 540x960, 24 fps, voiced) and `reference/contact-sheet.jpg`. Match it.

## Format
- Design 1, BrightPhotoDiagramEditorial. Background `#fff9ed linear-gradient(145deg,#e0f5e9,#fff9ed 70%)`.
- Headline at the top left in BOLD SANS, two to three short lines, the key phrase in the accent green (e.g. "Glycerin helps skin / hold water.").
- The middle is ONE visual that explains the point: a small labelled diagram (Glycerin -> Skin + water), a citation card
  (author et al. year, one line of what it studied, "not a product-performance score"), a not-equal diagram with nodes, or a real photo
  with a short tick list ("Directions", "Warnings"). Never a decorative shape.
- Narration as two-line TRANSPARENT subtitles in a lane of their own at about 77% of the height, centred, plain sans, no box.
- Four teaching scenes (Hook, Fact, Why, Solution) of about 4 to 7 s, then a separate centred official logo page with the exact line
  "Follow us for more" (>= 3.3 s). Voice: warm, plain English, brisk; music piano ducked under speech.
- Stock photo in an arch frame (rounded top), about 40% of the width.

## Always (all four formats, 26 Oct 2026)
- Background: a soft diagonal gradient only (see the reference frames). NEVER circles, blobs, bokeh, dots or other decorative shapes.
- No mascot, no talking head, no scene labels such as HOOK / FAKTA on screen. Flow stays Hook, Fakta, Kenapa, Solusi.
- Photos are real stock (Unsplash, credited in the row), in their own frame, never behind text; illustration only, never proof.
- Text never overlaps: every block has its own region; the subtitle lane is separate from the content.
- At least 15 of the 99 styles apply to every film (the worker chooses them per scene for fit; `ws-regulab-motion` holds the catalogue).
- Silent films are read at a quick reader's pace (about 2.6 words a second, a new fact every 3 to 4 seconds); narrated films are brisk.
- The base skill `ws-regulab-motion` (contract, catalogues, templates) applies on top of this format spec.
