## Format
- Design 1 with Bahasa Malaysia (Malaysian, never Indonesian: boleh, bukan bisa). Same gradient background as the English film.
- Scenes are visual-led: a real photo in an ARCH frame centred (hook), a small labelled diagram (Air / Gliserin / Kulit), a citation
  page ("Fluhr et al. (2008)" between thin rules, one plain sentence), a row of nodes with a not-equal sign, then the photo again with a tick
  list (Arahan, Amaran). Few words on the picture, in plain serif or sans, centred.
- Narration in short conversational sentences ("Boleh ke kita tahu...", "Belum tentu...", "Tapi...", "Jadi...") as two-line transparent
  subtitles near 77% of the height. Say "pembungkusan", not "label".
- Ends on the separate centred logo page with the exact line "Jangan lupa follow kami untuk tips seterusnya." (>= 3.3 s). Voice: Edge ms-MY,
  brisk; piano ducked.

## Always (all four formats, 26 Oct 2026)
- Background: a soft diagonal gradient only (see the reference frames). NEVER circles, blobs, bokeh, dots or other decorative shapes.
- No mascot, no talking head, no scene labels such as HOOK / FAKTA on screen. Flow stays Hook, Fakta, Kenapa, Solusi.
- Photos are real stock (Unsplash, credited in the row), in their own frame, never behind text; illustration only, never proof.
- Text never overlaps: every block has its own region; the subtitle lane is separate from the content.
- At least 15 of the 99 styles apply to every film (the worker chooses them per scene for fit; `ws-regulab-motion` holds the catalogue).
- Silent films are slow (about 1.4 words a second); narrated films are brisk.
