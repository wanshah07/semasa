# Production contract

## Designs are not recipes or effects
- BrightPhotoDiagramEditorial preserves the real-photo, original-diagram and typographic editorial language of v13 silent and v15 English/BM. Motion must explain an idea: crop/pan a real photo, reveal a relationship arrow, stage a statement or compare verified inputs. No generic arched bottle replacement or repeated slide layout.
- ContinuousLine preserves v17's approved slower v16 lineage. One connected tall SVG world and animated stroke, pen and camera, not disconnected scene cards. Mask connector strokes under label glyphs. The separate long outro is the only deliberate scene departure.
- Keep both selectable. Complete-motion recipes retain eight named source sections (seven distinct specification bodies; Client Testimonial duplicates Tutorial). Styles.txt retains original sections: Featured Effects, Hooks, Captions, Layouts, Proof, Visual Beats, Animated B-Roll, Signature Effects, Motion Pace, Sound, Colour & Finish. Catalog codes include a repeated H06 where the display says Freeze and Declare; do not invent H07 or a missing body.
- Only promote an effect to native support after a deterministic render with real prerequisites passes QA. An illustrative heading adaptation is not a full screenshot/matte/avatar implementation. Preserve absent/duplicate-code warnings. Missing required assets trigger a disclosed documented fallback or a blocker, not fake evidence.

## Story and language
Thirty-second default and optional sixty-second explainers: Hook → Fact → Why → Solution, actual evidence appropriate to the topic, separate outro. A silent script is independently rewritten into complete casual sentences with connected thought and reading holds. Count all simultaneously visible words and keep roughly ≤2 words/sec; reduce text rather than shrink the font. Ten-second gimmick requires explicit selection: one persistent scene, one curious truthful question, partial reveal, no full explanation or extra outro page.

Use complete conversational English or Bahasa Melayu Malaysia, not Indonesian or literal translation fragments. Malaysian cues include “Boleh ke…”, “Belum tentu…”, “Tapi…” and “Jadi…”. Rewrite mispronounced “label” as “pembungkusan” when the meaning allows. More than two authors: FirstAuthor et al. (year); explain briefly what the publication covers and how it supports the actual fact. Keep full URLs, study context, origin IDs and source scope in evidence sidecars. No claim to full-paper access when only an abstract was read. No quantitative chart without verified source values, units and study context.

## Voice and footage
Narration on/off, language and voice gender are independent settings. English approved moderate pace is approximately 154 active-speech WPM, not a universal speed slider. Existing English VoiceStudio Kitten expr-voice-2-f is a female preset, NOT a clone. If male requested, verify a supported male preset and render an audition; never use a silent placeholder or relabel the female preset. Actual v17 slowing was 90% of v16: Kitten native 1.50 and pitch-preserving atempo 0.936 equivalent (1.04 × .90), voice gain .9; arbitrary new scripts need measured/listened retiming. BM is Microsoft Edge ms-MY-YasminNeural (+15%, pitch +0Hz), not VoiceStudio, with supported ms-MY-OsmanNeural as a male option only after verifying availability. ElevenLabs excluded. No cloning assumptions or paid HeyGen uploads. Existing SadTalker experiment is not a production backend; lip-sync is not proved by animated stills.

For source footage: preserve voice, expression and meaning, continuing original picture beneath overlays; no dark full-frame cutaway hiding the subject. Protect eyes/mouth, allow useful torso-level diagrams. ASR cache keyed to original source hash, actual word timestamps and output-timeline offsets after cuts. Snap cuts to word boundaries with padding and short audio fades. Subtitles last, transparent/stable and readable, typically 2–4 words from real ASR. Do not distribute phrase duration evenly into fabricated word times. Obtain approval for edit strategy rather than blindly applying the explainer recipe.

## Sound, rights and QA
Approved original synthesized piano is a reusable cleared dependency with provenance. Gentle audible bed, fades, ducked under voice. Preserve note pitch. The v17 reference uses reel soundtrack/music gain ×.45 versus v16; its rights remain unverified/private-preview-only, not a portable/public default. Do not auto-download or package raw reel audio. Source preference alone does not mean its render route exists.

Run short native smoke before full render; cap workers/threads. Preserve approved originals via before/after hashes. Inspect encoded contact sheets AND close-spaced transitions, caption midpoints, first/end frames at phone size. Review layout rather than assuming matching palette is enough. Full FFmpeg decode and ffprobe required; audio measurements do not prove natural pronunciation or music taste. Archive only to authorized destinations, exact read-back after upload. No source repo changes, merges, deployment, paid service use or uploads implied by delivering a preview.
